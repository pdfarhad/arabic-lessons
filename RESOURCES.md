# Arabic Resources

## Knowledge

- [Book series site: Al-Arabiyyah Bayna Yadayk (العربية بين يديك)](https://www.arabicforall.net/)
  Publisher of the course book the class uses (library holds photos of Book 1 units 1–2).
  Use for: locating official audio and later volumes.
- [Wiktionary (English) — Arabic entries](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Category:Arabic_language)
  Vocalisation, morphology, gender and etymology per word; used as the dictionary-check
  source for every flashcard entry. Use for: verifying readings and glosses.
- [Madinah Arabic free course](https://www.madinaharabic.com/)
  Free structured MSA lessons that parallel Book 1's grammar points. Use for: a second
  explanation of gender agreement, demonstratives, possessive suffixes.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [r/learn_arabic](https://www.reddit.com/r/learn_arabic/)
  Active, well-moderated learner community with native speakers. Use for: "did I say this
  naturally?" checks and study-method questions.
- Local: the user's weekly Arabic class.
  Use for: speaking practice and teacher feedback — the primary community; the class owns
  the syllabus.

## Gaps

- Official ABY Book 1 audio recordings (the printed dialogues have studio audio somewhere;
  browser TTS is the stand-in until found).
