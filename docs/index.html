Arabic — Bayna Yadayk Companion

أَهْلاً وَسَهْلاً! A spoken-first companion to a taught Arabic class using Al-Arabiyyah Bayna Yadayk, Book 1. Each unit gets a lesson (grammar frames + practice), a dialogue reader (line-by-line audio), and a flashcard deck; topics that cut across units get a chapter of their own, and the book's review tests become practice papers.

1. Lessons

  1. 1Greetings & Introductions Meet, greet, ask names and origins — correctly gendered 20m
  2. 2The Family Family words, my/his suffixes, first verbs, في + place 20m
  3. 3Housing Where you live, I want…, renting and furnishing, noun + adjective 20m
  4. 4Daily Life When?, the verbs of the day (I / you / you f.), سَـ will, لا don't, the clock, by bus 20m

2. Topic chapters

  1. 1Numbers and Their Forms Digits, counting, counting things, ranking, and the days — one number, five costumes 25m
  2. 2Prepositions — the placing words في · مِنْ · إِلى · لِـ · عَلى · بِـ · مَعَ · لَدَى · عِنْدَ · بَعْدَ — where and when, with gap-fill practice; grows with each unit (now Units 1–4) 30m

3. Dialogue readers

  1. 1Unit 1 Dialogues Six dialogues, line-by-line: speak, hear, reveal 15m
  2. 2Unit 2 Dialogues Family photo · the Prophet's ﷺ family tree · fajr at home 15m
  3. 3Unit 3 Dialogues Where do you live · renting a flat · the furniture shop · the days 15m
  4. 4Unit 4 Dialogues A school morning · the family's day off · a holiday morning 15m

4. Flashcards

  1. 1Unit 1 Vocabulary Deck 50 words, both directions, with audio 10m
  2. 2Unit 2 Vocabulary Deck 41 new words, both directions, with audio 10m
  3. 3Unit 3 Vocabulary Deck 54 new words — housing, furniture, counting, ranking, days 12m
  4. 4Unit 4 Vocabulary Deck 42 new words — the verbs of the day, time words, the clock, things at home 12m

5. Practice papers

  1. 1Exam 1 — the paper after Units 1–4 The book's own 40-mark review test, exercise for exercise: join, group, odd-one-out, brackets, reading — marked as you go 35m

6. Reference

  1. §Frames & Phrasebook (Units 1–4) Every phrase, frame, agreement rule and number form — printable

Suggested rhythm per unit: lesson → flashcards → dialogue reader → flashcards again a day later. Questions go to your teacher — in class, or to your study agent when reading in a live session.
