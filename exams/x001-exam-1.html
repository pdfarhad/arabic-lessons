2. One new shape: he does it

Units 3 and 4 gave you every verb of the day as I and you. The paper writes the same verbs about Ahmad. Watch the front letter — press ▶ or step with ‹ ›.

أَغْسِلُ المَلابِسَ

أَغْسِلُ — I wash the clothes. Unit 4 p.2. The front letter أَ is “I”.

تَغْسِلُ المَلابِسَ

تَغْسِلُ — you wash the clothes. Unit 4 drill 8. Same word, new front letter.

يَغْسِلُ المَلابِسَ

يَغْسِلُ — he washes the clothes. This paper. The front letter is the person; nothing after it changes. Unit 2 already did this with يَقْرَأُ / تَقْرَأُ.

مَتى

السّاعَةَ

meaning

Ahmad wakes up early, at dawn. He prays fajr in the mosque. Ahmad does not sleep after the fajr prayer; he reads the Qur'an. He goes to school by bus at seven o'clock. On the day off he wakes up early too. On Friday, in the morning, he reads a newspaper or a book, and he prays the Friday prayer in the big mosque.

Print vs speech: the paper prints يَوْمُ العُطْلَةِ and يَوْمُ الجُمُعَةِ with -u. As time words (“on the day off”, “on Friday”) Unit 4 and item ٣ below print يَوْمَ with the -a that the Prepositions chapter calls the missing preposition. The 🔊 says يَوْمَ; treat the -u as a print slip.
