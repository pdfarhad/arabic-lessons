١٠عَشَرَةعَشْرُ …العاشِر / العاشِرَة—

Rules: 3–10 take the opposite gender of the counted noun (drop the ة before a she-noun, keep it before a he-noun: ثَلاثَةُ أَوْلادٍ) and the noun goes plural. Ranking words follow the noun, both with الـ, and agree normally. كَمْ takes a singular with ـًا (كَمْ غُرْفَةً؟). Week names count from Sunday: الأَحَد (1) … الخَميس (5); السَّبْت = rest, الجُمْعَة = gathering. Units 1–3 use rows 1–5 of the "before a she-noun" and ranking columns; rows 6–10 there, ثَماني/عَشْرُ, and the 1–2 phrases are beyond the book. Full walk-through in the numbers chapter.
