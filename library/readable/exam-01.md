## أَوَّلاً: المُفْرَداتُ (p.1) — Vocabulary, 20 marks

### 1. صِلْ بَيْنَ الشَّيْءِ والمَكانِ الَّذي يوجَدُ فيهِ. — 6 marks

| --- | --- |
| ١- فُرْن | أ- غُرْفَةُ جُلوس |
| ٢- سَرير | ب- حَمّام |
| ٣- أَريكَة | ج- مَطْبَخ |
| ٤- مِرْآة | د- غُرْفَةُ نَوْم |
| ٥- كِتاب | هـ- مَسْجِد |

## ثانِياً: التَّراكيبُ والنَّحْوُ (p.2) — Structures & grammar, 13 marks

### 1. صِلْ بَيْنَ جُزْأَيِ الجُمْلَةِ. — 8 marks

Join the two halves of each sentence.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| ١- يَغْسِلُ | أ- الجُمُعَةَ في المَسْجِدِ |
| ٢- يُشاهِدُ | ب- في حَيِّ الجامِعَةِ |
| ٣- يَذْهَبُ إِلى | ج- الصَّحيفَةَ |
| ٤- يَكْنُسُ | د- المَدْرَسَةِ |
| ٥- يَقْرَأُ | هـ- الغُرْفَةَ |
| ٦- يُصَلّي | و- المَلابِسَ والأَطْباقَ |
| ٧- يَسْكُنُ | ز- التِّلْفازَ |
| ٨- يَتَوَضَّأُ | ح- لِلصَّلاةِ |

`[key]` ١ يَغْسِلُ → و المَلابِسَ والأَطْباقَ · ٢ يُشاهِدُ → ز التِّلْفازَ · ٣ يَذْهَبُ إِلى → د
المَدْرَسَةِ · ٤ يَكْنُسُ → هـ الغُرْفَةَ · ٥ يَقْرَأُ → ج الصَّحيفَةَ · ٦ يُصَلّي → أ الجُمُعَةَ في
المَسْجِدِ · ٧ يَسْكُنُ → ب في حَيِّ الجامِعَةِ · ٨ يَتَوَضَّأُ → ح لِلصَّلاةِ.
(Unit 4 drills 1 and 7 give the verb–object pairs; Unit 3 p.1 the district; Unit 2 p.3
the wuḍūʾ.) The endings do the work: the ـِ after إِلى / في rules out every ـَ object.

### 2. أَكْمِلْ بِوَضْعِ الكَلِمَةِ الصَّحيحَةِ مِمّا بَيْنَ القَوْسَيْنِ. — 5 marks

Complete with the correct word from the brackets.

1. في الشَّقَّةِ أَرْبَعُ .......... (غُرَف / غُرْفَة)
2. كَمْ .......... في الشَّقَّةِ؟ (حَمّام / حَمّاماً)
3. .......... تَذْهَبُ إِلى المَدْرَسَةِ؟ (مَتى / ماذا)
4. أَذْهَبُ .......... السّابِعَةَ. (السّاعَةَ / ساعَةَ)
5. هَذِهِ .......... الشَّقَّةُ. (هُوَ / هِيَ)

`[key]` ١ غُرَف — أَرْبَعُ غُرَفٍ, the plural after 3–10 (Unit 3 p.2 خَمْسُ غُرَفٍ; c001) ·
٢ حَمّاماً — كَمْ + one thing with ـاً (Unit 3 p.2 كَمْ غُرْفَةً; c001) · ٣ مَتى — the
answer is a time, and ماذا would need an object (Unit 4 p.1) · ٤ السّاعَةَ — the clock
phrase is always definite (Unit 4 p.1, drill 6; c002 §5) · ٥ هِيَ — الشَّقَّة is
feminine (Unit 2 p.3 هَذِهِ هِيَ …).

## ثالِثاً: القِراءَةُ (p.2) — Reading, 7 marks

اِقْرَأِ الفِقْرَةَ، ثُمَّ أَجِبْ بِنَعَمْ (✓) أَوْ لا (X).

> يَسْتَيْقِظُ أَحْمَدُ مُبَكِّراً عِنْدَ الفَجْرِ. يُصَلّي الفَجْرَ في المَسْجِدِ. أَحْمَدُ لا يَنامُ
> بَعْدَ صَلاةِ الفَجْرِ، هُوَ يَقْرَأُ القُرْآنَ. يَذْهَبُ إِلى المَدْرَسَةِ بِالحافِلَةِ السّاعَةَ
> السّابِعَةَ. يَوْمُ العُطْلَةِ، يَسْتَيْقِظُ مُبَكِّراً أَيْضاً. يَوْمُ الجُمُعَةِ في الصَّباحِ يَقْرَأُ
> صَحيفَةً أَوْ كِتاباً، وَيُصَلّي الجُمُعَةَ في المَسْجِدِ الكَبيرِ.

1. يَسْتَيْقِظُ أَحْمَدُ مُبَكِّرًا. ( )
2. يُصَلّي أَحْمَدُ الجُمُعَةَ في المَسْجِدِ الكَبيرِ. ( )
3. يَوْمَ الجُمُعَةِ يَقْرَأُ صَحيفَةً أَوْ كِتاباً. ( )
4. أَحْمَدُ يَنامُ بَعْدَ صَلاةِ الفَجْرِ. ( )
5. يُصَلّي أَحْمَدُ الفَجْرَ في البَيْتِ. ( )
6. أَحْمَدُ مُهَنْدِسٌ. ( )
7. يَذْهَبُ إِلى المَدْرَسَةِ بِالسَّيّارَةِ. ( )

`[key]` ١ ✓ · ٢ ✓ · ٣ ✓ · ٤ X (لا يَنامُ) · ٥ X (في المَسْجِدِ) · ٦ X (nothing in the
text makes him an engineer; he goes to school) · ٧ X (بِالحافِلَةِ).
Item 6 is the one that cannot be matched to a sentence — it tests that "not said" is لا.

## Transcription notes

- **p.2, passage** — «يَوْمُ العُطْلَةِ» and «يَوْمُ الجُمُعَةِ» are printed with a ḍamma on
  يَوْم. Both are time adverbs ("on the day off", "on Friday"), and for exactly that use
  Unit 4 p.3 prints «يَوْمَ العُطْلَةِ» with a fatḥa — as does this paper's own item 3
  («يَوْمَ الجُمُعَةِ يَقْرَأُ…»). The ـَ is the shape c002 §5 teaches as "the missing
  preposition". Kept as printed in the passage; read it as a print slip, and say يَوْمَ.
- **p.2, passage vs item 1** — مُبَكِّراً (tanwīn on the alif) in the passage, مُبَكِّرًا
  (tanwīn on the ر) in item 1. Same word, two typesetting conventions.
- **p.2, passage** — «بِالحافِلَةِ» is only partly vocalised in print; transcribed with
  Unit 4 p.1's full vowels.
- **p.1, §3 item 4** — «ثَلّاجَة» printed with the shadda; Unit 3's card spells it
  ثَلاجَة without. Wiktionary has ثَلَّاجَة; the shadda is right and the card could carry it.
- **Marks** — 6 + 7 + 7 + 8 + 5 + 7 = 40, matching the printed «= ٤٠». No fourth
  (writing) section on these two pages, and the total closes, so nothing is missing.
- No other slips found.

## What this paper drags in (flagged at ingest)

1. **The he-form (يَـ) of the Unit 3–4 verbs — recognition.** يَغْسِلُ، يُشاهِدُ، يَذْهَبُ،
   يَكْنُسُ، يَسْكُنُ، يَسْتَيْقِظُ، يَنامُ، يَكْوي appear here for the first time; the class
   pages had them only as أَ… / تَ… (I / you). Unit 2 p.3 established يَـ = he for three
   verbs (يَتَوَضَّأُ، يُصَلّي، يَقْرَأُ). The paper never asks the learner to *produce* a
   he-form — matching (§ثانياً 1) and reading (§ثالثاً) are recognition — so this is
   grammar appearing in a text, not a syllabus move. Teach it as the swap it is: the
   أَ / تَ at the front becomes يَـ, the rest of the word does not change.
2. **Narrative retelling of a dialogue.** The passage is Unit 4 dialogues 1 and 3 fused
   into one third-person paragraph about أَحْمَد (the dialogues' speakers were طارِق /
   طاهِر and عادِل / فَيْصَل). The skill tested is dialogue "I" → report "he", plus
   هُوَ يَقْرَأُ (pronoun + verb) and لا يَنامُ (Unit 4's لا + verb, now on a he-form).
3. **لِلصَّلاةِ "for the prayer".** Unit 3 flagged لِـ + الـ fusing to لِلـ (لِلمَطْبَخِ); c002
   already teaches the fusion. New here: the purpose sense, يَتَوَضَّأُ لِلصَّلاةِ. Same
   "for"; a new attestation for the c002 ledger, not a new row.
4. **كَمْ + a masculine noun.** كَمْ حَمّاماً? — Unit 3's كَمْ غُرْفَةً rule on a word that
   ends in a consonant, so the ـاً is written with an alif. The distractor (حَمّام) is the
   rule's absence. Likewise أَرْبَعُ غُرَفٍ reuses Unit 3's counted-noun plural.
5. **Category words used as instructions.** أَثاث (Unit 3) is now a heading; غُرَف
   (rooms), أَعْدادٌ تَرْتيبِيَّة (ordinal numbers), أَماكِن (places), أَفْعال (verbs),
   اسْتِفْهام (question words), أَعْداد (numbers) are new. Grammar-book labels the learner
   must recognise to answer §أولاً 2 — nothing to say in conversation.
6. **Rubric language.** Section names المُفْرَدات، التَّراكيب والنَّحْو، القِراءَة; the
   imperatives صِلْ (join), أَضِفْ (add), ضَعْ (put), أَكْمِلْ (complete), اِقْرَأْ (read),
   أَجِبْ (answer); and الشَّيْء، المَكان، كَلِمَتَيْن، مَجْموعَة، عَلامَة، الكَلِمَة الغَريبَة،
   جُزْأَي الجُمْلَة، القَوْسَيْن، الفِقْرَة، نَعَمْ / لا. The first imperatives in the course.
   Recognition only, and exam-only: they do not belong in any unit's word set.
7. **Odd-one-out is category recall.** §أولاً 3 needs the learner to hold the Units 1–4
   words by class: professions, rooms, verbs, appliances, ordinals vs cardinals,
   dwellings vs furniture, places vs time.
8. **Endings as clues.** §ثانياً 1 can be solved from the last vowel alone (ـِ after إِلى /
   في, ـَ on objects) — the shapes c002 and Lesson 3 teach as sounds, now doing work.
