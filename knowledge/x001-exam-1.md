- **Claim.** The paper is the book's review test after Units 1–4: 40 marks in six
  exercises — vocabulary 6 + 7 + 7, structures & grammar 8 + 5, reading 7 — the marks
  being the printed tick-box numbers and the total the printed «= ٤٠».

- **Claim.** Every content word on the paper is attested in Units 1–4 (each is in a unit's
  word set or transcription); the he-forms and the rubric lines are the only Arabic not on
  a class page.
  — ingest check 2026-09-04 against assets/vocab-l01…l04.js and library/readable/lesson-01…04.md
- **Claim.** The exercise types are: thing → place matching; add two words to a category;
  odd one out; verb → complement matching; choose the right form from brackets; a
  third-person paragraph with نَعَمْ / لا items. The paper prints no answer key.
  — exam-01.md §§ أولاً–ثالثاً
- **Claim.** ⟂ recognition — the he-form: the front letter يَـ turns a Unit 3–4 verb from
  I / you to he, and nothing after it changes: أَغْسِلُ / تَغْسِلُ / يَغْسِلُ. Eight verbs
  appear this way for the first time (يَغْسِلُ، يُشاهِدُ، يَذْهَبُ، يَكْنُسُ، يَسْكُنُ،
  يَسْتَيْقِظُ، يَنامُ، يَكْوي); Unit 2 already had يَقْرَأُ، يُصَلّي، يَتَوَضَّأُ. The paper
  asks only for recognition (matching, reading).
  — exam-01.md, drag-in flag 1; lesson-02.md p.3 and drill 3; lesson-04.md drills 1, 8
- **Claim.** The vowel on the front letter travels with the person: أَغْسِلُ → يَغْسِلُ (a),
  أُشاهِدُ → يُشاهِدُ (u). Wiktionary's conjugation of شاهد gives يُشَاهِدُ; of غسل, يَغْسِلُ.
  — https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/شاهد ; https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/غسل (2026-09-04)
- **Claim.** تَـ is two people: هِيَ تَقْرَأُ *she* reads (Unit 2) and تَسْكُنُ *you* live
  (Unit 3) are the same form; context decides.
  — lesson-02.md drill 3; lesson-03.md p.1
- **Claim.** The reading passage is Unit 4 dialogues 1 and 3 retold in the third person
  about one boy, أَحْمَد; the dialogues' speakers were طارِق / طاهِر and عادِل / فَيْصَل.
  Item ٦ (أَحْمَدُ مُهَنْدِسٌ) matches no sentence: a statement the text does not make is لا.
  — exam-01.md ثالثاً + key; lesson-04.md pp.1, 3
- **Claim.** Print slip: the passage prints يَوْمُ العُطْلَةِ and يَوْمُ الجُمُعَةِ with -u where
  Unit 4 p.3 and the paper's own item ٣ print the time adverb يَوْمَ with -a (c002 §5,
  "the missing preposition"). The page keeps the print, speaks يَوْمَ, and says so.
  — exam-01.md transcription notes; lesson-04.md p.3; Wiktionary يوم (accusative يَوْمَ "on the day of")
- **Claim.** Grammar tested from the brackets: plural after 3–10 (أَرْبَعُ غُرَفٍ); كَمْ +
  one thing with ـاً, written with an alif on a consonant-final word (كَمْ حَمّاماً); مَتى
  for a time answer; the clock phrase definite (السّاعَةَ السّابِعَةَ); هَذِهِ … هِيَ for a
  feminine noun.
  — lesson-03.md p.2 (خَمْسُ غُرَفٍ، كَمْ غُرْفَةً); lesson-04.md p.1, drill 6; lesson-02.md p.3; c001, c002 §5
- **Claim.** لِلصَّلاةِ "for the prayer": لِـ fused onto الـ (Unit 3's لِلمَطْبَخِ), here in the
  purpose sense — يَتَوَضَّأُ لِلصَّلاةِ. Same "for"; a new attestation for c002's لِـ, not a new
  row.
  — exam-01.md ثانياً 1; lesson-03.md p.3; c002 §3
- **Claim.** ⟂ recognition — the rubric: أَوَّلاً / ثانِياً / ثالِثاً section numbers;
  المُفْرَدات vocabulary; التَّراكيب والنَّحْو structures and grammar; القِراءَة reading; and
  the first command forms in the course — صِلْ join, أَضِفْ add, ضَعْ put, أَكْمِلْ complete,
  اِقْرَأْ read, أَجِبْ answer. Exam-only; never carded in a unit's set.
  — exam-01.md, drag-in flag 6; Wiktionary وصل, أضاف, وضع, أكمل, قرأ, أجاب (2026-09-04)
- **Claim.** Dictionary catches (Wiktionary, 2026-09-04): bare نحو is "side, direction"
  before "grammar"; فقرة is "vertebra" before "paragraph"; bare أكمل lists the elative
  "more complete" before the verb "to complete"; كنس is "to hide, retreat" before "to
  sweep"; كوى is "to sear, cauterize" before "to iron"; استفهام is "interrogative,
  question"; مفردات is the plural of مُفْرَد "single (word)"; ثَلّاجَة carries the shadda
  the paper prints and the Unit 3 card omits.
  — https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/{نحو,فقرة,أكمل,كنس,كوى,استفهام,مفردات,ثلاجة}
