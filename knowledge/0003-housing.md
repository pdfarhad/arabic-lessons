# Knowledge: Housing

> Distilled from lesson [0003](../lessons/0003-housing.html).

- **Claim.** Unit 3 adds the I / you (m.) present-tense forms: أَسْكُنُ / تَسْكُنُ,
  أُريدُ / تُريدُ; the prefix أـ marks "I", تـ marks "you (m.)" — and تـ is also
  "she" (Unit 2), so تَسْكُنُ is ambiguous out of context.
  — Lesson 3 photos (library), Unit 3 p.1, p.5 drills 2–3; Wiktionary سكن، أراد
  (checked 2026-08-30)
- **Claim.** The thing wanted after أُريدُ takes the ending ـًا (ـةً on feminine
  nouns): أُريدُ شَقَّةً، سَريرًا، فُرْناً. Taught as a shape, not as case.
  — Lesson 3 photos, Unit 3 pp.2–3, p.5 drills 1, 5; MISSION.md excludes i'rab theory
- **Claim.** ماذا is "what?" before a verb (ماذا تُريدُ؟); ما (Unit 1) before a noun.
  — Lesson 3 photos, Unit 3 p.3
- **Claim.** لِـ "for" attaches to the following noun and fuses with الـ into لِلـ:
  لِغُرْفَةِ النَّوْمِ، لِلمَطْبَخِ، لِلحَمّامِ.
  — Lesson 3 photos, Unit 3 p.3; Wikipedia *Arabic grammar* § Prepositions (لِـ "to, for")
- **Claim.** An adjective follows its noun and agrees in gender by the ة rule:
  بَيْتٌ جَميلٌ / شَقَّةٌ جَميلَة. First noun + adjective phrases in the course.
  — Lesson 3 photos, Unit 3 p.2, p.5 drills 8–9
- **Claim.** كَمْ "how many" takes a singular noun with ـًا; the answer uses number +
  plural (كَمْ غُرْفَةً؟ — خَمْسُ غُرَفٍ). Detail in the numbers chapter.
  — Lesson 3 photos, Unit 3 p.2, p.5 drills 4, 7
- **Claim.** أَيّ "which" (في أَيِّ دَوْرٍ؟) is answered with an ordinal after the noun,
  both definite: في الدَّوْرِ الخامِسِ.
  — Lesson 3 photos, Unit 3 p.2, p.5 drills 6, 10
- **Claim.** رَقْم is a label-number (flat 5, house 9), read with the plain counting
  word; ـكَ "your" (Unit 1's اسْمُكَ) attaches to any noun, ة opening to ت (شَقَّتِكَ).
  — Lesson 3 photos, Unit 3 p.1
- **Claim.** Fixed frames of the unit: لَدَيْنا "we have"; أُريدُ مُشاهَدَةَ … "I'd like
  to see …"; أَيَّ خِدْمَةٍ؟ "how can I help?"; تَفَضَّلْ / اُدْخُلْ; مِنْ فَضْلِكَ;
  شُكْراً (also = no thanks); بَعْضَ الأَثاثِ; شَيْئاً آخَرَ.
  — Lesson 3 photos, Unit 3 pp.2–3; Wiktionary تفضل، لدى (checked 2026-08-30)
- **Claim.** Two print quirks: p.2 «أُريدُ مُشاهَدَةَ الشَّقَّةِ؟» is a statement printed
  with a question mark; p.3 «أَيَّ خِدْمَةٍ.» is a question printed with a full stop.
  Reproduced as printed in the reader, annotated beside the line.
  — Lesson 3 photos, Unit 3 pp.2–3; transcription notes in library/readable/lesson-03.md
- **Claim.** Dictionary catches: bare حي is "alive" first (neighbourhood second); bare
  دور is "role, turn" first (storey later); آخر is آخَر "other" or آخِر "last"; كم is
  كَمْ "how many" or كُمّ "sleeve"; أي is أَيّ "which" or أَيْ "i.e.".
  — Wiktionary حي، دور، آخر، كم، أي (checked 2026-08-30)
- **Claim.** Days of the week, Saturday first: السَّبْت، الأَحَد، الإِثْنَيْن (book:
  الإِثْنَين)، الثُّلاثاء، الأَرْبِعاء، الخَميس، الجُمْعَة.
  — Lesson 3 photos, Unit 3 p.4; Wiktionary entries for each (checked 2026-08-30)
