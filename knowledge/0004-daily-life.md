# Knowledge: Daily Life

> Distilled from lesson [0004](../lessons/0004-daily-life.html).

- **Claim.** Unit 4's question word is مَتى "when?"; it is answered by a time phrase, not
  a clock number alone: عِنْدَ الفَجْرِ، بَعْدَ الصَّلاةِ، في الصَّباحِ، السّاعَةَ السّابِعَةَ،
  يَوْمَ العُطْلَةِ، مُبَكِّراً / مُتَأَخِّراً.
  — Lesson 4 photos (library), Unit 4 pp.1, 3; Wiktionary متى "when (at what time?)"
  (checked 2026-09-02)
- **Claim.** The I / you (m.) prefixes from Unit 3 (أَ / تَ) apply to ten new verbs:
  أَسْتَيْقِظُ، أُصَلّي، أَنامُ، أَفْعَلُ (only تَفْعَلُ printed), أَقْرَأُ، أَذْهَبُ، أَكْنُسُ، أَغْسِلُ،
  أَكْوي (I only), أُشاهِدُ. أُصَلّي and أَكْوي end in ي and show no -u.
  — Unit 4 pp.1–3, p.4 drills 1, 7–8; Wikipedia *Arabic verbs* § Regular verb
  conjugation (ʔa- 1sg, ta- 2sg prefixes, read 2026-09-02)
- **Claim.** The you-form addressed to a woman takes the ending ـينَ: تَفْعَلينَ،
  تَكْنُسينَ، تُشاهِدينَ (drills 3–4; سَتَفْعَلينَ يا فاطِمَةُ p.2). First feminine "you" verb in
  the course; taught as a frame, no paradigm (learning record 0001).
  — Unit 4 p.2, p.4 drills 3–4; Wikipedia *Arabic verbs* (paradigm row تَكْتُبِينَ 2sg f.)
- **Claim.** The future is the prefix سَـ on the present-tense verb: سَأَكْنُسُ، سَتَفْعَلُ،
  سَتَفْعَلينَ.
  — Unit 4 p.2, p.4 drills 2, 4; Wikipedia *Arabic verbs* § Tense ("adding … the prefix
  سَـ sa- … onto the beginning of the present tense verb")
- **Claim.** لا in front of a present-tense verb negates it: لا أَنامُ "I don't sleep"
  (Unit 3's لا was only "no").
  — Unit 4 p.1
- **Claim.** Clock time is السّاعَة + the feminine ordinal, both definite: السّاعَةُ
  الواحِدَة … السّادِسَة (drill 6), السّابِعَة (p.1). "At seven" is السّاعَةَ السّابِعَةَ with
  no preposition — the -a ending does the job (taught as a shape; c002 §5).
  — Unit 4 p.1, p.4 drill 6; Wiktionary ساعة "hour"; c001 knowledge note
- **Claim.** Definite noun + definite adjective: المَسْجِدُ الكَبيرُ; after في both end -i,
  في المَسْجِدِ الكَبيرِ. Unit 3 had only the indefinite pair (شَقَّةٌ جَميلَة).
  — Unit 4 p.3
- **Claim.** A definite object ends -a: أَقْرَأُ القُرْآنَ، أَكْنُسُ الغُرْفَةَ، أَغْسِلُ
  المَلابِسَ — Unit 3's ـًا shape on a word with الـ. Sound only, no case theory
  (MISSION.md).
  — Unit 4 p.1, p.4 drills 1, 7
- **Claim.** بِـ + vehicle = "by": بِالسَّيّارَةِ، بِالحافِلَةِ. إِلى + destination with a verb:
  أَذْهَبُ إِلى المَدْرَسَةِ. Detail in the prepositions chapter.
  — Unit 4 p.1, p.4 drill 5; c002 knowledge note
- **Claim.** Fixed lines of the unit: هَذا يَوْمُ العُطْلَةِ / يَوْمُ العَمَلِ; أَقْرَأُ صَحيفَةً أَو
  كِتاباً (أَو = or); أُصَلّي الجُمُعَةَ (the day-name for its prayer); … أَيْضاً (also,
  sentence-final); يا + name ending -u (يا طارِقُ).
  — Unit 4 pp.2–3; Wiktionary أو، أيضا، الجمعة (checked 2026-09-02)
- **Claim.** Print points: الجُمُعَة is vowelled with a ḍamma on م here and a sukūn in
  Unit 3's strip (both readings listed on Wiktionary); بِالسَّيّارَة's final vowel is too
  faint to read on p.1 (transcribed with -i from the pattern); drill 6 prints السّاعَة
  unvowelled.
  — library/readable/lesson-04.md transcription notes
- **Claim.** Dictionary catches: bare أصلي is أَصْلِيّ "original" first; bare أنام / أقرأ /
  أذهب resolve to form-IV headwords (put to sleep / make read / make disappear) — the
  book's are form I; ثانية alone is "a second" of time; كتاب is listed as the verbal noun
  "writing" first; بعد is the verb "to be far" first; عمل the verb "to work" first;
  مدرسة "school" is spelt like مُدَرِّسَة.
  — Wiktionary via scripts/check-vocab.py (run 2026-09-02)
