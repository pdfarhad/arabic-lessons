# Knowledge: The Family

> Distilled from lesson [0002](../lessons/0002-the-family.html).

- **Claim.** Unit 2's family nouns come in gender pairs: والد/والدة، جد/جدة، عم/عمة،
  ابن/ابنة (with أخ/أخت from Unit 1); عمّ is the paternal uncle specifically.
  — Lesson 2 photos (library), Unit 2 pp.1–2; Wiktionary عم (checked 2026-08-23)
- **Claim.** The unit introduces exactly two possessive suffixes: ـي "my" (والدي) and
  ـهُ "his" (والدُهُ); the full paradigm is out of scope until the class reaches it.
  — Lesson 2 photos, Unit 2 p.5 drill 6; scope decision in learning record 0001
- **Claim.** ة opens to ت before suffixes: والدة ← والدتي/والدته، عمة ← عمته،
  ابنة ← ابنته.
  — Lesson 2 photos, Unit 2 pp.1–2, p.5 drill 6
- **Claim.** Present-tense verbs gender by prefix: يـ he / تـ she — يقرأ/تقرأ،
  يصلي/تصلي، يتوضأ/تتوضأ; lemma توضأ = "to perform ablution".
  — Lesson 2 photos, Unit 2 p.3, p.5 drill 3; Wiktionary توضأ (checked 2026-08-23)
- **Claim.** The unit's sentence shape is person + في + place + verb:
  سعيدٌ في الغرفةِ يقرأُ القرآنَ.
  — Lesson 2 photos, Unit 2 p.3, p.5 drill 4
- **Claim.** هذا هو / هذه هي + definite noun means "this is THE one" (pointing out),
  distinct from plain هذا/هذه introductions.
  — Lesson 2 photos, Unit 2 p.3, p.5 drill 5
- **Claim.** مَنْ "who?" and مِنْ "from" share one spelling; the vowel distinguishes
  them (Wiktionary lists both under بare من).
  — Lesson 2 photos, Unit 2 p.5 drill 2; Wiktionary من (checked 2026-08-23)
- **Claim.** Fixed Islamic-register phrases of the unit: ما شاء الله (admiration),
  صلى الله عليه وسلم (after naming the Prophet), الله أكبر (adhan), هيا بنا (let's
  go), vocative يا + name/title.
  — Lesson 2 photos, Unit 2 pp.1–3
- **Claim.** Numbers 6–10: ستة، سبعة، ثمانية، تسعة، عشرة.
  — Lesson 2 photos, Unit 2 p.4
- **Claim.** نظارة (glasses) is grammatically singular — one pair; bare spelling's
  first Wiktionary sense is "telescope", the vowelled نَظّارَة is the glasses word.
  — Lesson 2 photos, Unit 2 p.3; Wiktionary نظارة (checked 2026-08-23)
